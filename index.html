Your chosen retrieval tool drastically changes your flight approach:

  • Robotic Grapple Arm: Fires a high-velocity mechanical cable directly forward. Clamps onto a single debris item and reels it into your hold. Lowest battery consumption and yields a +35% Scrap Bonus.
  • Wide-Angle Capture Net: Launches an expanding electromagnetic energy mesh that encloses all small/medium debris pieces within its blast radius simultaneously. Great for clearing dense fragment clouds.
  • Magnetic Tractor Beam: Hold SPACEBAR to emit a continuous graviton cone. Continuously pulls all metallic debris in front of you into your intake. High battery drain.

Earth's orbit contains multiple classes of hazardous debris:

  • Structural Bolt & Shards (15kg): Small, fast-moving metal fragments from shattered rocket skins.
  • Broken Solar Array (45kg): Fragile photovoltaic panels rich in rare earth materials.
  • Research Cubesat (75kg): Miniaturized satellites carrying high-value avionics.
  • Spent Rocket Upper Stage (220kg): Massive propulsion tank left drifting. High scrap yield.
  • Derelict Comms Satellite (380kg): Heavy telecommunication platforms with intact dishes.
  • Prototype Fusion Core (160kg): Rare experimental modules with enormous credit bounty.

Aegis Depot Recycling: When your cargo hold fills up, fly back to the central orbital station. Docking safely unloads all items, converts them to Score & Scrap Credits, refuels your tanks, and restores full hull integrity!

Watch out for dangerous orbital threats and heed the Collision Warning Radar:

  • Kinetic Micrometeoroids: Hypersonic projectiles crossing orbit. Severe collision damage.
  • Volatile Hydrazine Mines: Explosive pressurized fuel tanks. Heavy explosive damage on ram.
  • Defunct RTG Nuclear Satellites: Cold War satellites emitting continuous radiation fields that rapidly drain battery systems.
